Check our work

Answers, error counts and the exact launch command are kept per run. Open a model and read its data checks.
    The wallThe set of models that no other model beats on both axes at once. Moving toward it costs you either speed or quality; beyond it nothing exists on this card.
    95% intervalThe range the true score very likely sits in, given how many questions were asked. Overlapping intervals mean the order between two models is not settled.
    Watt-hours per correct answerEnergy the card drew, measured at the board, divided by the number of questions answered correctly. Lower is better.
    Cut offThe share of answers that hit the token limit before the model finished. A cut-off answer scores zero.
